# Front page models

- `player-talk.glb` — the player (a boy in a headset) of the front page. Generated with Meshy (meshy.ai) from
  Yury's turnaround of the splash art's boy, rigged and animated by Meshy (Idle, Talk_with_Right_Hand_Open,
  Wave_One_Hand); the «mouthOpen» morph added in Blender (`~/code/02_vr-projects/xr-player/blender/mouth.py`).
  Compressed with gltf-transform (meshopt geometry, WebP 1024 texture; 5.7 MB → 0.9 MB; the skeleton, the clips
  and the mouth morph kept). Made for this project; Meshy's terms apply to generated assets.
